Game Vault
Setup guide

Make it yours in about five minutes

Game Vault keeps your collection in a Google Sheet that you own. There is no account to create and nothing to install. You need a Google account and a computer for the setup; after that it works on your phone.

1Copy the sheet

  1. Open the Game Vault sheet and choose Make a copy. The copy is yours alone.
  2. On the Players tab, type your two player names and pick a dinosaur and color for each.
  3. On the Games tab, replace the sample rows with your games. Only the Game column is required.
Copy the Game Vault sheet Download as a spreadsheet file

The one-click copy link is being prepared. Until then, download the spreadsheet file, upload it to Google Drive, open it with Google Sheets, then add the script: in the sheet choose Extensions > Apps Script and paste in the contents of Code.gs.

2Switch on the connection

  1. In your sheet, choose Extensions > Apps Script.
  2. Click Deploy > New deployment. Click the gear icon and choose Web app.
  3. Set Execute as to Me and Who has access to Anyone. Click Deploy.
  4. Click Authorize access and choose your account.
  5. Copy the Web app URL. It ends in /exec.

Google shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" notice for any script you copy yourself. That is expected: the script belongs to you and runs only inside your own sheet. Choose Advanced, then Go to Game Vault, then Allow. It asks to see and edit only the spreadsheet it is attached to.

3Connect the app

  1. Open Game Vault and go to Settings.
  2. Paste the Web app URL and tap Connect. Your collection replaces the sample games.
  3. To add your phone or another person, tap Copy full-access link or Copy family link in Settings and open that link on the other device.
  4. Optional: in Settings, use the BoardGameGeek card to add a game by name or import the games you own on BoardGameGeek. Player counts, scores, and covers are filled in for you.

On iPhone, an app added to the home screen keeps its own settings. Add Game Vault to your home screen first, open it from there, and paste the invite link into its Settings.

?How your data is protected

  • Your collection stays in your Google account. The app has no server and no database of its own.
  • The first device to connect becomes the owner. After that, the web app URL alone does nothing; every device needs a key that only you can hand out.
  • Family links never receive prices or notes. People using one can browse and log plays, and that is all.
  • You can cut everyone off. In your sheet, choose Game Vault > Reset access keys, then connect again.
  • Do not use "Publish to web". The app does not need it, and it would make that tab public.
