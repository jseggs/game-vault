Player Avatars

The Players sheet controls each fighter. Player #1 and #2 supply the display name, dinosaur, and color used throughout the app.

Dinosaurs: T-Rex, Velociraptor, Triceratops, Ankylosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Pterodactyl, Spinosaurus, Stegosaurus. Colors: cyan, gold, pink, purple, lime, orange, teal, or red.

BoardGameGeek

Add a game by name, or bring in your whole BoardGameGeek collection. Player counts, scores, categories, and covers are filled in for you. Only your search or BGG username goes to the lookup service. Your sheet is never shared with it.

Games already in your vault are kept as they are. Only empty BoardGameGeek columns are filled in.

Make it yours

Game Vault is free and open source. Your collection lives in a Google Sheet that you own; this app is only the screen in front of it. Setup takes about five minutes: copy the sheet, switch on its connection, and paste its web app URL above.
