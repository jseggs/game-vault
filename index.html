Your board games, in a sheet you own

Game Vault turns a Google Sheet into a collection app for your shelf. Browse your games, let it pick tonight's, log who won, and watch two pixel dinosaurs settle the score.

Try the demo Set up your own

The demo runs on a sample collection. Nothing to sign up for, nothing saved.

What it does

Built for the question "what are we playing tonight?"

Four screens, each doing one job. All of them work in the demo.

Stage 1 · Collection

See the whole shelf at a glance

Every game with its cover, player count, BoardGameGeek score, and your own ratings.

  • Filter by shelf, player count, played or unplayed
  • Track how much of the collection you have played
  • Collection value, visible only to the owner
Stage 2 · Game picker

Let it make the call

Set the table rules, press start, and get one game. No more twenty minutes in front of the shelf.

  • Narrow by shelf, player count, or never played
  • A two-player mode that knows which games shine at two
  • Keep a game out of two-player picks with one tap
Stage 3 · Play log

Log a play in a few taps

Pick the game, pick the winner, add a note if you like. Each player can rate the game while it is fresh.

  • Plays are written straight to your sheet
  • Ratings update the game's row
  • Works from any phone you have invited
Stage 4 · High scores

Settle it with a dino duel

Your head-to-head record, staged as an arcade fighter. Each player picks a dinosaur and a color.

  • Total wins and the last five results
  • Per-game win records and each player's favorites
  • Eight dinosaurs to choose from
On your phone

Add it to your home screen

Game Vault is a web app. There is nothing to download from an app store. Open it on your phone, add it to the home screen, and it behaves like any other app.

Fill it from BoardGameGeek

Add a game by name, or import the games you own on BoardGameGeek by username. Player counts, scores, categories, and covers come along.

Share with the family

Send an invite link. A family link can browse and log plays, and never sees what you paid.

How it works

About five minutes, no code

You need a Google account and a computer for the setup. After that it runs on your phone.

1

Copy the sheet

One click makes your own copy of the Game Vault sheet. Type your player names and your games.

2

Switch on the connection

The sheet carries a small script. Deploy it from the sheet's menu and Google gives you a private address.

3

Connect the app

Paste that address into Game Vault. The first device becomes the owner and can invite the rest.

What you own

Your data never leaves your Google account

There is no Game Vault server holding your collection. The app is a screen in front of your own sheet.

The sheet is yours

Edit it by hand, add columns, export it, or delete it. If Game Vault disappeared tomorrow, you would still have everything.

The script sees one spreadsheet

It asks Google for access to the sheet it lives in and nothing else. Not your Drive, not your other sheets.

Every device needs a key

The sheet is never published to the web. Lost a phone? Reset the keys from the sheet's menu.

The code is open

Read it, change it, host your own copy. BoardGameGeek lookups send only a search phrase or a BGG username.

Press start

Poke around the demo first. If it fits your table, the setup guide walks you through the rest.
